This line chart compares the percentage of cardiac rehabilitation patients meeting the weekly exercise goal of 150 minutes across four follow-up visits. The x-axis shows visits 1 through 4, and the y-axis shows the percentage of patients meeting the goal from 0 to 100%. The orange dashed line with square markers represents Home-based patients, while the blue solid line with circular markers represents Center-based patients. At visit 4, 70% of Home-based patients and 79% of Center-based patients met the exercise goal, a difference of 9 percentage points. These results describe an association and do not prove that either rehabilitation program caused better outcomes.
